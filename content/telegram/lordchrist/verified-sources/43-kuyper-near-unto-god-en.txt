Verbatim (contiguous excerpt):

Blessed is the man whom thou choosest, and causest to approach unto thee, that he may dwell in thy courts (Ps. 65:4). The psalmist joins together what men so often put asunder: election and drawing near. No man comes near to God unless God chooses him and draws him; and no man is chosen of God to remain at a distance from him. Calling, election, drawing near are links of one chain of grace.

Drawing near to God is no mere matter of the intellect or of mystical rapture; it is the motion of the whole man -- mind, will, heart, conscience, daily life -- that begins in God and ends in God. We draw near, not because we of ourselves have aspired to something higher, but because he has eternally loved us in Christ and predestinated us unto adoption through Jesus Christ.

It is good for me to draw near to God (Ps. 73:28) -- not only in the life to come, not only in hours of rapture, but in darkness, in temptation, in the ordinary round of life; not only when we feel his presence but when his face is hidden; not only in victory but in inward struggle -- it is good for me to draw near to God, for apart from him there is no salvation, no rest, no life.
