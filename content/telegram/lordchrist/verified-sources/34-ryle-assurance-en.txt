Verbatim (contiguous excerpt):

He looks downward to the grave, and he does it without fear. Hear what he says: I am ready to be offered. I am like an animal brought to the place of sacrifice, and bound with cords to the very horns of the altar. The drink-offering ... is already being poured out. The last ceremonies have been gone through. It only remains to receive the death-blow, and then all is over. The time of my departure is at hand. I am like a ship about to unmoor and put to sea. All on board is ready.

These are remarkable words to come from the lips of a child of Adam like ourselves! Death is a solemn thing... The grave is a chilling, heart-sickening place, and it is vain to pretend it has no terrors. Yet here is a mortal man who can look calmly into the narrow house appointed for all living, and say, while he stands upon the brink, I see it all, and am not afraid.

Let us hear the Apostle once more. He looks forward to the great day of reckoning, and he does it without doubt... Henceforth there is laid up for me a crown of righteousness, which the Lord, the righteous Judge, shall give me at that day: and not to me only, but unto all them also that love His appearing... The Apostle speaks without any hesitation or distrust... He speaks as if he saw it all with his own eyes.
