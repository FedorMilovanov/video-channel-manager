# Milovi Cake Telegram — portfolio-first copy style

Owning issue: #353.  
Project: `milovi-cake`.  
Status: provider-inert editorial standard.

## Core voice

A post published by Milovi Cake already carries Milovi Cake authorship. Copy should therefore describe the cake, dessert, occasion, visible design choices or useful selection context directly. Do not waste the caption proving that the work is "real", "ours" or "from our gallery" unless provenance itself is the editorial subject.

The default voice is calm, visual, confident and useful. It should read like a premium portfolio/media channel, not like an internal asset catalogue, a dataset annotation or a Pinterest note.

## Default caption shape

1. Short human title, optionally with one relevant emoji.
2. One paragraph about visible form, palette, composition, texture, theme or format.
3. Optional useful context: how a customer might think about palette, theme, size/format or personalisation. Do not invent order history, ingredients, price, availability, customer reaction or production process.
4. Fixed public-resource footer from `editorial-public-footer-2026-08.json`.
5. Zero to four relevant hashtags. No hashtag walls.

## Language to avoid

Do not use service/catalogue phrases such as:

- `реальная работа Milovi Cake`;
- `реальная работа` / `наша реальная работа`;
- `на фото — реальная работа`;
- `из нашей галереи` when it adds no reader value;
- `хороший референс`;
- `вот пример торта`.

Also avoid repetitive openings like `На фото...` when the visual is already visible above the caption.

## What to say instead

Describe what is visible and why the composition works. Examples of legitimate editorial angles:

- one leading character keeps a children's design coherent;
- a restricted palette makes floral decoration feel collected rather than noisy;
- repeated colours tie separate cupcakes into one set;
- 3D form can carry the main theme without stacking unrelated details;
- a smaller dessert format may suit a more intimate table.

These are visible/design observations, not claims about an unseen kitchen process.

## Resource footer

The default footer is intentionally centralized. It keeps public destinations out of the body and prevents links from being forgotten:

```text
🎂 Milovi Cake — торты и десерты на заказ
📍 Санкт-Петербург

🌐 Сайт: https://milovicake.ru/
💬 ВКонтакте: https://vk.ru/milovi_cake
▶️ YouTube: https://www.youtube.com/@milovi_cake
📖 Дзен: https://dzen.ru/milovicake.ru
```

Telegram does not need to link to itself inside every Telegram post.

## Safety boundaries

- Finished-work media is not BTS/process evidence.
- Milovi School is a separate editorial stream and does not prove Cake recipes, techniques or production lineage.
- Customer quotations remain exact and source-bound.
- Price, delivery, timing, availability and service claims require fresh exact support when used.
- Editorial approval is not provider execution authority.
