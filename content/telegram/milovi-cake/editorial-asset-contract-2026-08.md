# Milovi Cake Telegram — editorial asset contract

Date: 2026-08-15
Owning issue: #353
Status: **provider-inert editorial constraint**.

This contract records the currently available source material for Telegram planning. It is authoritative over older launch-pack or growth-playbook examples whenever they imply production footage that does not exist in reviewed source assets.

## Current usable source assets

Milovi Cake currently has a substantial corpus of real finished-work media:

- finished cake photographs;
- finished cake videos;
- close-up/details that can be cropped from verified finished-work media;
- verified customer reviews;
- factual product/order information from Milovi Cake;
- sourced pastry-culture material from Milovi School.

## Currently unavailable / prohibited assumptions

Until separately reviewed source footage exists, do **not** plan, promise, stage or publish:

- kitchen footage;
- kitchen-tour content;
- behind-the-scenes / BTS production footage;
- staged "how we made this cake" sequences;
- fabrication/assembly/decorating process footage;
- invented before/after production stories;
- copy that implies viewers will receive production access which the source corpus cannot support.

The current kitchen is not an editorial asset. Do not expose it merely to satisfy a content mix.

## Launch content mix while this contract is active

Use the existing real-media archive as the strength of the channel:

- **45% finished-work showcase** — strongest cakes, real photos/videos, clean searchable first lines;
- **20% finished-work detail/design breakdowns** — composition, colour, form, focal detail, visual idea, using only what is visible or documented;
- **10% collections and native polls** — real works grouped by occasion, palette, format or theme;
- **10% verified social proof and buyer guidance** — exact reviews, brief/order guidance, delivery/order facts after freshness checks;
- **10% Milovi School mini-stories** — short sourced pastry-culture/history pieces;
- **5% factual commercial posts** — only real current products/offers, with no invented scarcity or availability.

Production BTS / kitchen share: **0%** until this contract is deliberately revised against reviewed source footage.

## Safe finished-media formats

Preferred reusable formats include:

- one cake / several verified angles;
- short finished-cake video or loop;
- macro/detail crop from an existing asset;
- "detail of the week" from a real work;
- a collection of real cakes for one occasion/theme/palette;
- A/B/C preference poll backed by real gallery assets;
- finished-work design commentary limited to visible/documented facts;
- verified review paired with a relevant real work only when the mapping is actually known;
- buyer guidance paired with a neutral real cake image.

## Language rule

In older drafts, words such as "process" must not be interpreted as production/kitchen footage while this contract is active. They may refer only to the documented **ordering/selection process** unless an exact reviewed production asset is attached.

Before live publication, any ambiguous wording must be rewritten so a reader is not promised BTS or kitchen access.
