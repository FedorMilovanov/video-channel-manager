# Milovi Cake Telegram — editorial brand boundary

Updated: 2026-08-16
Owning issue: #353
Project: `milovi-cake`

This document is a durable editorial boundary for every new Milovi Cake Telegram draft, release, caption, growth experiment and adaptation.

## Milovi Cake is the product brand

Milovi Cake is the cake and dessert business. Public product copy may describe only products, services, designs, formats, facts and claims supported by Milovi Cake sources.

Do **not** describe Milovi Cake as a French-cuisine or French-pastry business merely because Milovi School contains material about French pastry culture. Do not infer a recipe, technique, kitchen practice, culinary lineage, training method, ingredient policy, production process or product origin from Milovi School content.

## Milovi School is a separate editorial / educational project

Milovi School is a separate educational and content project used for useful reading, editorial depth, SEO/discovery and brand-interest content. Its articles may be adapted only as separately labelled educational reading.

Milovi School content is **not evidence** that Milovi Cake:

- makes its cakes from the School's recipes;
- uses the School's techniques or production methods;
- has a French kitchen, French product identity or French culinary heritage;
- trains its production team through the School;
- derives current Milovi Cake products from a School article or historical pastry tradition.

Any direct Cake ↔ School production or product claim requires a separate exact source that explicitly proves that relationship.

## First-screen rule

The first ten launch posts for `@MiloviCake` contain **zero Milovi School posts**. The first screen must establish Milovi Cake itself through real finished work, practical customer guidance, exact social proof and clear navigation.

After the first screen, School material may appear occasionally only when:

1. it is explicitly labelled as separate educational/article content;
2. the copy does not imply that the article describes how Milovi Cake products are made;
3. it is useful to a reader even without a purchase CTA;
4. any quotation or factual product claim still has its own exact source.

## Other hard editorial rules

- No invented customer stories, reviews, ingredients, availability, prices, delivery promises or production claims.
- No staged/fake production BTS while reviewed production footage does not exist.
- No first-person Victoria voice unless an exact source or explicit reviewed approval supports that wording.
- Finished-work analysis must stay within what is visible or documented; do not infer unseen process details.
- Historical provider payloads remain immutable evidence. If a bad payload was actually sent, preserve its digest and record a correction/supersession instead of rewriting history.

## Superseded canary wording

The historical canary `milovi-cake-canary-001` used wording that connected Milovi Cake with “French pastry culture from Milovi School.” That exact payload was sent once and subsequently deleted by the channel owner because the framing was misleading.

That historical caption is evidence only and is **do-not-republish**. It must never be used as a template for future Milovi Cake positioning.
