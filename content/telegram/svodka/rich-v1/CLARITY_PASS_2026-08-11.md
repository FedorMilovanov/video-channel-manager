## Why this pass exists

The successful native Rich Message canary proved that Telegram can preserve a
complex article containing inline media and structural rich blocks. It also
exposed an editorial risk: a capability test can look like a demonstration of
formulas, tables and widgets instead of a publication written for an ordinary
reader.

Production Svodka articles now follow a different rule: explain the scientific
idea first, then use Rich Message capabilities only where they reduce reader
confusion or make a mechanism easier to see.

## What changed across all 14 articles

- technical concepts are introduced through a plain-language mental model
  before specialist terminology;
- measurements say what was measured and why the number matters;
- analogies are used only where they preserve the underlying mechanism;
- claim boundaries are made explicit so recognition does not become detailed
  recollection, survival does not become normal life, lineage age does not
  become an unchanged modern species, and local temperature does not become
  total energy;
- media purposes/captions were rewritten around explanation rather than
  decoration;
- formulas/tables are not used as production ornaments;
- source sets and recorded evidence boundaries remain the authority for facts;
- JSON and Markdown companions were rewritten together;
- every article remains `editorial_draft_review_required` and
  `provider_writes_authorized: false`.
