## Primary witness and editorial reasoning

- English passage: Stephen Charnock, *The Complete Works*, vol. I (Edinburgh: James Nichol, 1864), printed p. 172, *A Discourse upon the Existence of God* (Ps 14:1).
  Facsimile/OCR transcription: https://www.digitalpuritan.net/Digital%20Puritan%20Resources/Charnock%2C%20Stephen/The%20Complete%20Works%20of%20Stephen%20Charnock%20%28vol.1%29.txt.html
- A second transcription: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/53527
- The phrase 'strange crossing of some in their estates' describes adverse reversals in people's economic/property circumstances. The earlier Russian wording «странное расстройство у некоторых имений» wrongly suggests landed estates themselves are disordered.
- 'the whole design of men's preservation' concerns measures people devised to preserve themselves, not an abstract «замысел человеческого сохранения».
- 'as if they rationally designed it' is a simile: circumstances converge **as though** they had a rational design. The Russian proposal does not imply that circumstances themselves can reason.

The proposed rich-text q-10 preserves the English sequence of examples, its distinct concluding bold span, attribution, all other blocks, sources and media. This is **not** a signed independent translation review or a rights clearance. The 1864 printing is not asserted to be an original 17th-century facsimile.
