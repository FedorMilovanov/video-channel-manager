# Ownership, editorial authority and third-party rights

**Repository maintainer and technical operator:** [Fedor Milovanov](https://github.com/FedorMilovanov). Video Channel Manager coordinates auditable media workflows across separate projects. The work uses human-directed AI-assisted engineering and editorial tools; repository ownership does not mean every script, image or commentary was created without those tools.

## Responsibility is project-specific

- **Technical engineering and operations:** Fedor Milovanov maintains the repository implementation, safety protocols, tests, release controls and tool integrations. The operator may review a proposed action, but that **does not authorize provider execution** without the exact target, preflight, consent and postconditions required by [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md).
- **Separate channels and brands:** The `lord-god-strength`, `legendary-poet`, and `milovi-cake` project keys identify different editorial and provider targets, not interchangeable credentials or content. The canonical identity and account bindings belong to [docs/operations/project-identity-registry.md](docs/operations/project-identity-registry.md).
- **Confectionery products:** Milovi Cake is associated with Viktoria Milovanova's confectionery business. The presence of the business's media channel in this repository does not transfer authorship of its cakes, photographs, reviews or products to the technical maintainer.
- **Media and external works:** Original speakers, performers, poets, publishers, source photographers and rights holders retain their applicable rights. A locally indexed video, quoted text, source snapshot or generated editorial image does not become owned or freely redistributable merely because an API returned it. Provider terms remain applicable.
- **AI-generated materials:** AI-assisted draft copy, analysis, prompts and images are subject to source verification, editorial approval, provenance/rights review and platform-specific rules before any publication. AI output is not itself a documentary witness.
