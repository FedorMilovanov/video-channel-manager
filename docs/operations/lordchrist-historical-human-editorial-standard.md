# LordChrist historical human-editorial standard

Status: required for every historical Telegram production release after the rejected 2026-09-07 v1 canary.

## Reader-facing rule

A historical post must read like a compact historical essay written by a careful pastor-editor, not like a search result, fact-check memo, source audit, or AI research summary.

The reader-facing body must lead with the event itself: people, place, date, decision, conflict, consequence. Internal verification language belongs in claims/bindings and source notes, not in the main prose.

## Required substance

Each post must contain:

1. at least three concrete historical anchors drawn from bound evidence: dates or dated periods, named people/institutions, places, documents, decisions, actions, or measurable consequences;
2. a clear chronological or causal thread instead of generic thematic description;
3. at least one close primary-source anchor when the source corpus permits it: an exact short quotation, a dated document, a letter, a court/archive record, a published contemporary statement, or an equivalent first-hand record;
4. enough context to explain why the event mattered to the people involved, not merely what later writers say about it;
5. an organic pastoral/theological application that grows from the event and Scripture, without turning the historical actor into an infallible authority;
6. a compact source block after the essay. Source grading and uncertainty language may remain available there or in internal evidence structures, but must not dominate the article.

## Reader-facing anti-patterns

Do not write the body around phrases or headings such as:

- “источники показывают”;
- “современное исследование рассматривает” as a substitute for the event itself;
- “где заканчивается документ и начинается интерпретация”;
- “что можно доказать документами” as the main hook;
- “в этой версии цикла…”;
- descriptions of the editorial/research process;
- repeated disclaimers that consume space better used for concrete history.

Qualification is still mandatory when evidence is limited, but it should be local and natural: state exactly what is uncertain in one sentence and continue the narrative.

## Tone

Write in natural Russian. Prefer active verbs, named subjects, chronological movement, restrained vivid detail, and sentences a human editor would actually publish. Avoid inflated drama, invented interior thoughts, sentimental embellishment, stock AI transitions, and generic moral conclusions.

The application should sound like pastoral instruction, not a compliance note. It may be firm. For LordChrist, it should remain aligned with the repository theology profile: Scripture is inspired and sufficient; salvation is by grace through faith in Christ; historical actors and traditions are evaluated under Scripture.

## Release gate

A transport-green canary is not editorial approval. If the owner rejects the reader-facing copy, that publication identity remains consumed for idempotency/audit purposes, the rejected Telegram message may be removed by the owner, and the production cycle must remain held until a new publication identity and exact release binding are reviewed.
