## Decision summary for Milovi

1. **First growth mechanism:** useful archive + source-attributed owned-surface acquisition, not paid scale.
2. **Core free attribution feature:** one Telegram invite link per source/partner/placement.
3. **Core post-purchase trust loop:** `tg-box` QR plus independent official Yandex review QR.
4. **Core Telegram product choices:** public channel, searchable natural Russian headlines, Direct Messages, occasional native polls with a real follow-up.
5. **Core content moat:** real Milovi work + Victoria + verified reviews + School source trail; do not manufacture generic AI “cake content”.
6. **Dzen:** cheap secondary distribution until measured evidence earns manual work.
7. **Paid Telegram seeding:** evaluate geography, recent reach, dynamics and audience quality; unique invite link for every placement.
8. **VK:** keep as a primary commercial/growth surface; test channel/creator/video tools only with source-level measurement.
9. **Local reputation:** Yandex profile quality, honest reviews and official promo tools deserve priority because Milovi is geographically constrained.
10. **Legal:** own informational content and paid promotion are not interchangeable categories; check exact circumstances before any paid collaboration.
